## 📝 Full Product Description

### Stop context-switching between five different apps just to read your books.

If you own books in different formats — a PDF from Gumroad, an EPUB from Amazon, an MP3 audiobook from Audible — you're probably juggling multiple apps, losing your place, and wasting time every day just getting started.

**Sonara fixes that.** It's a single, beautiful Windows desktop app that brings every format you own into one smart library — so you can read, listen, highlight and take notes without ever switching apps.

---

### What you get with Sonara

#### 📚 One library for every format
Import PDF, EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, MP3, M4A and M4B files. Sonara automatically detects if you have the same book in multiple formats and groups them into a single card — click to choose whether you want to read or listen.

#### 🎧 A real audiobook player
Full chapter navigation, variable speed (0.5× to 3×), seek bar, and a persistent mini-player that floats above your other windows while you work. Control playback from the system tray without switching windows.

#### 📖 A powerful eBook reader
Paginated PDF and EPUB reading with colour-coded, persistent text highlighting. Three themes (black, white, blue), adjustable font size, and line spacing. Your place is saved automatically — to the exact word.

#### ✨ AI that actually helps
Connect your Claude API key and chat with the AI about any chapter you're reading. Ask for explanations, summaries, or deeper context — right inside the app without copy-pasting into a browser.

#### 🔊 Text-to-speech for any page
Built-in TTS with Microsoft neural voices reads any section of your eBook aloud. Skip markdown noise, chapter headers and custom words you define. Great for commuters and accessibility needs.

#### 🗂️ Smart genre collections
One click of **Classify All** and Sonara uses AI to sort your entire library into genre collections — Science Fiction, Biography, Self-Help and more. Or create your own collections and drag books in manually.

#### ☁️ Sync that doesn't need a subscription
Drop the database file into your Dropbox, OneDrive or Google Drive folder and your progress, highlights and notes are automatically synced to every Windows PC you use. No Sonara account required.

## ❓ FAQ

**Does it work without internet?**
Yes. Reading, listening, highlighting, notes and progress are all 100% offline. Internet is only used if you enable AI chat or Turso cloud sync.

**Do I need a subscription to keep using it?**
No. This is a one-time purchase. Updates included for the lifetime of the v2 product line.

**Can I use my existing audiobooks?**
Yes — any MP3, M4A or M4B file you have on your computer can be imported.

**Does it strip DRM?**
No. Sonara only reads files that your device can already open. It does not circumvent any copy protection.

**Which AI model is used?**
Claude by Anthropic. You bring your own API key (free tier available at console.anthropic.com). Your key is stored only on your device.

**Will there be a Mac version?**
The codebase is cross-platform (Electron). A Mac build is on the roadmap.
